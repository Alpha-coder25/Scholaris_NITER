What is Scholaris?

Today, course materials travel over email and Facebook groups, exams happen on paper, grading has no analytics, and research supervision lives in inboxes. Scholaris replaces all of that with one structured, auditable platform — and puts a human in the loop wherever AI is involved.

📚

Course materials

Versioned lecture uploads, scoped to enrolled students only.

🤖

AI question bank

AI drafts questions from your materials — a teacher reviews and approves every one.

⏱️

Server-enforced exams

One question at a time with per-question timers enforced by the server — no tampering.

⭐

Private ratings

Faculty feedback stays private and aggregated — never a public leaderboard.

How it works

Three roles, one loop — from course assignment to a graded, analysed exam.

Admin
  1. 1. Defines departments, semesters and the course catalog
  2. 2. Assigns a teacher to each course offering
  3. 3. Monitors institution analytics & aggregated ratings
Teacher
  1. 1. Uploads lecture materials
  2. 2. Generates AI-drafted questions → reviews & approves them
  3. 3. Builds a timed exam, schedules the window
  4. 4. Grades written answers, views per-question analytics
Student
  1. 1. Enrolls in courses for the semester
  2. 2. Reads materials, takes server-timed exams
  3. 3. Sees MCQ scores instantly, full results after grading
  4. 4. Rates faculty — privately and anonymously

How to log in

  • 1 Students — press “Sign up as a student” above (or use the demo account below). After sign-up you're logged in automatically and can enroll in courses.
  • 2 Teachers & Admins — accounts are created by the institution. Log in with the credentials you were given (or use the demo accounts below).
  • 3 Everyone lands on a role-specific dashboard after logging in.
